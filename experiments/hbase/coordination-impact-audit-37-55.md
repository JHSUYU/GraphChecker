# HBase findings 37–55: coordination cause and exception impact

Audited 2026-09-19 against HBase `1790942bb2d46f41f1b4aed94db85e6f5bd18a1a`.
Scope is the existing 19 ledger entries only. A **pass** requires a branch relation
between separately maintained coordination states, an exception caused by that
relation, and failure of a meaningful public request or service operation (or a
source-deterministic critical startup path). **Insufficient** means the observed
effect or contract does not prove one of those requirements. **Fail** means the
specific exception is expected independently of the coordination bug, is caught
locally, is an expected failure before a separate side-effect bug, is fully
retried by the critical worker, occurs after the relevant request already
failed for another reason, or is only a storage-layer exception without the
claimed critical impact.
Novelty is separate; pass does not mean a new, unreported root cause.

All 19 test files and targeted Surefire XML reports exist. Test totals are
13 green suites, 6 deliberately red suites, all with `errors=0`; red assertions
are stated below rather than misrepresented as passing regressions. The tests
were not rerun during this audit; source and current report artifacts were read.
