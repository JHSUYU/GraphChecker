## (c) Tools and offline method (all read the run only)

- `A/compose-run.sh <tag> <jar> <case out dir> [heapMB]`: runs ComposeMain over `schedule/iteration-0000/sessions` with the run's anchor, writing `A/compose/<tag>/{candidate.smt2,manifest.json,compose.log}`. Heap is 8–16 GB; HP8-15 takes about 7 min. Run on lift11 only. Use a copied jar (`A/probe/reask-N.jar`) so a gate rebuilding `target/` doesn't change it under you.
- `A/plan-run.sh <side> <jar-ABSOLUTE> <case out dir> <tag> <depth> [heapMB]`: one offline ExploreMain planner round over iteration-0000, every owed arm. The jar path must be absolute.
- `A/case-evidence.sh <case out dir> <tag>`: the re-ask pipeline (plan at depth 0 and 1, `ReaskProbe` queries, `z3phase.py`, `summary.py`).
- `tools/analysis/vacuity.py [--latest] [--detail] <round dirs...>`: says whether an UNSAT is vacuous. Outputs are `A/vacuity-latest.tsv` and `A/vacuity-latest-detail.txt`.
- Probes in `A/probe/src` (package-private access, compiled against a jar: `javac -cp reask-N.jar -d classesN`):
  - `SeedProbe`: dumps body, valuation-0 pins, clock pins, recorded pairings, own arms.
  - `MoversProbe`: movers of an EXPR in a session.
  - `ZkProbe`, `ArmProbe`, `ReaskProbe`.
- Direct z3 on a candidate: strip `(check-sat)`, then add `(assert <path_selector>)` plus your pins or equalities and `(check-sat)`. This is how the HZ17 and HP8-15 mechanisms were proved.
- Gate: `env -u BASH_ENV tools/onlift.sh -h lift11 -g 10 bash -c 'mvn -q -o package && bash src/test/shell/run_engine_campaign_helpers_test.sh'`. The PLANNER-LIVE fixture has a 10 s wait and failed once under load (gate-12); the rerun (gate-12b) passed.

## (d) Non-obvious engine lessons, with evidence

- **Vacuous UNSAT**: an UNSAT_CLOSED is often vacuous. vacuity.py found 08, 15, nsrg, 34 and 31 vacuous because no root and no read in the guard's slice offered another writer; 37's fatal arm was literal false. Check this before counting an UNSAT.
- **FORMULA_SAT on the seed path, two causes:**
  1. A free clock that is not a root (HZ17-mat, fixed by 208ac1899).
  2. A havoc leaf in phi whose recorded value nothing pins (HP8-15, open).

  In both, `ClaimPolicy#closed` names a claim that moves nothing. CONFIRM refutes it ("moves nothing" / WIRE), so the cost is one CONFIRM per claim, but the formula is unfaithful.
- **Unplaced clocks**: placement used to require the same node. A cell read only by re-runs whose other cells were on other nodes refused nearly every path, and the arms that kept it left it free. Solr 19 is the same class of problem (41 of 44 refused) and has not been rerun on 208ac1899.
- **Doors-only occurrences with no CLOCK mint**:
  - HZ13-mat: a retried dispatch planted or exported a clock without a mint row, so the seed path got UNPRODUCIBLE_ANSWER.
  - H2's tree workaround (`masterSystemTime`, gc/hb-zk 1c1abcc349) removed master0's seed clock cells, and that is what left T0 unplaced in HZ17.
  - A workaround that removes recorded readings can unplace replay-only cells.
- **Deeper-world dedup limits**:
  - The canonical key removes name noise only. 18413: 4103 OK texts were all distinct; canonical keys answered 2633 asks from 1470 keys. Arms asked under more than one key: 33 of 397.
  - On 39, cost is dominated by build refusals (5839 REFUSED, 6.8 h of builds), not by solving.
  - A re-run must not count as a deeper world (HZ14-34, fixed by aff46402a).
- **`SmtLowering.forSession` vs the Composer's lowering**: their repair predicates differ (forSession counts every written location as re-pairable). Movers and other answers measured with forSession are not the Composer's.
- **Remote exit 255**: 26 such exits on lift06/07 killed replays (HZ14-mat RERUN 220f96b359b583ca). Reported to the lead; cause not found.
