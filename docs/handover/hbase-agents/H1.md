## (d) HBase traps, each with evidence

1. **Node identity must be the same in every run.** Hostnames are localhost. Ports increase in node order (the first is random, so concurrent replays on one host don't collide). Start codes are 1700000000000 + 1000*k (CausynthNodes, tree 32b2e6a249). ServerName spellings must be stated before the node's first ZK write (CausynthHBaseCluster#spell, 4d3cc2d197); otherwise you get DECLARATION_MISSING on start codes (HZ9-24).
2. **hbase:meta placement.**
   - Meta goes to whichever RS becomes a destination first: its first load report (ServerManager#createDestinationServersList, HBASE-25032), not its registration.
   - The destination list came from a HashSet whose order follows the port-dependent hash.
   - Fix: register in node order with the turn advanced at the first load report, sort the destinations, and have BaseLoadBalancer place in server order (4d3cc2d197 + c246d3fe1c). Meta is now on rs0 in every run.
   - Evidence: HZ9-24 recording had meta on rs2 and the replay on rs1. The client's meta Gets then went to other nodes and the fallback ExecProcedure stood on another response's anchor (OCCURRENCE_ANCHOR_UNMATCHED). HZ12-24 still had meta on rs2 with only the registrations ordered.
3. **Procedure steps (CausynthStep).**
   - The first step is handed off at push. Each later step is handed off at the end of the previous step, inside its claim, under the procedure IdLock (da04e47d03 + H3's 464505d240).
   - My first version filed the hand-off after the claim had closed, which gave WAKE poll#k addresses (R10-31).
   - Waking a step from whoever pushed it (an RPC reply callback, a timeout thread) gave reply-chain addresses no replay reproduces (R8-31).
   - Steps are filed at a do-nothing SynchronousQueue executor so they are HANDOFF_TASKs that CONFIRM holds where they run (1dda23ecc5). As QUEUED_TASKs submitted by a root thread they were ORDER_UNHELD (HP7-15).
   - Residual: a step that only meets a busy lock (LOCK_EVENT_WAIT) or yields still counts as a step.
4. **WAL.** DisabledWALProvider is set by class name for every WAL, and the meta provider ignores WAL_ENABLED (bffff8ca68, owner ruling).
   - With no WAL, DisabledWAL never clears a roll request, so the rollers spin after their period: NEVER_MS for the roll periods and the memstore periodic flushes (055d37dc25).
   - Earlier, the WAL coalescing consumer caused 91 stalls in the seed replay (HP2).
5. **Chores.** A slow replay reaches periodic chores (balancer, catalog janitor, hbck, normalizer, cleaners, meta's 5-min flush) that the recording never reached (HZ-24, H2's f9340f2aab turns them off). A check whose race partner is a chore must drive that chore from its workload.
6. **Client pairing.** In one JVM the test (EXTERNAL_APP, auto0/client0) reads and writes the master's objects directly. PLAN's DemandWalk and EXPLORE's static candidate writers must pair across nodes when either side is a client, with object identity deciding (6f22d129f, 83174452b; manifest `clientNodes`). Without this, 15's writers were never planned (HP4/HP5 NO_ROOT, then HP7 planned 620 tasks).
7. **Enums.** A held enum constant must be named of its class (EnumConstantName). Before that, `state == State.OPEN` stated no condition (HP4, literal false). No silent enum compare: equals(constant name, value) or REF_OPERAND_UNSTATED (vm-force12, 2a9dfbc2c; RegionEnumSmoke covers each shape).
8. **Sorted maps.** TreeMap, CSLM, TreeSet, CSLS and their views/wrappers are a named bound `ordered_map_unmodeled` in result.json `bounded` (03a125a20). RegionStates and Result maps are sorted, so 13 and 14 hit it.
9. **Infrastructure kills.** A remote replay whose worker ends at 255 (its head-side ssh client ended) or above 128 is re-queued at most twice (aa43d46df).
   - HZ14-mat lost 24 replays at once when ssh clients on lift11 were ended; lift06's sshd logged mass "disconnected by user" from lift11 at 20:40:14.
   - Run heads only on your own hosts, detached in their own sessions.
10. **Ledger and hosts.**
    - A planner head charges ~23 GB for its life. With 2 heads on a 3-host group, replays starve (HP9-13: REPLAY 1138 s, 10 "waiting for room").
    - With the 14-host pool, a host the head cannot reach in batch mode fails PREFLIGHT (HP10-14: lift15 from lift03).
    - `onlift -g 12` tree builds also take ledger room while runs are going.
11. **Other.**
    - A gate run concurrent with another mvn in the same worktree fails spuriously (gate8): never run two mvn in one worktree.
    - The tree's Gson 2.9 must be dropped from the workload classpath; the runtime needs Gson 2.10 (classpath.py).
    - Use `HBASE_MAVEN_REPO=/bigtemp/vnr3ne/m2/hbase-platform` inside the onlift command.
