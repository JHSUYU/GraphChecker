## Stopping a lane (learned 2026-09-13)
`$OUT/pgid` written by final/accept_final.sh is the harness bash's pgid, but `timeout` puts the campaign in its own process group, so `kill -- -$(cat pgid)` reports "no such process" and kills nothing. Stop a lane by SESSION id (setsid makes pid == sid): `pkill -TERM -s $(cat $OUT/pgid)`, wait, then `pkill -KILL -s …`; confirm with `ps -eo sid= | awk '$1==SID' | wc -l` → 0. Kill only when the campaign is provably futile (e.g. flip-solver JVMs spinning in the encoder for >1 h with empty stdout); the on-disk corpus (REPLAY/IMPORT/finished waves) stays usable for offline repro and recompose.
